How It Works

Four simple steps to smarter hiring

1. Define Requirements: Add required skills to each job position in your recruitment settings.

2. Candidates Apply: Applicants select their skills from an intuitive dropdown menu in the application form.

3. Auto-Matching: System intelligently matches skills and automatically routes qualified candidates to appropriate stages.

4. Hire Faster: Focus your time interviewing only pre-qualified candidates with matching skill sets.

Enhanced Application Experience

Modern, user-friendly interface for applicants

Candidates enjoy a seamless application experience with intuitive skill selection dropdowns, real-time form validation, and mobile-responsive design. The system guides applicants through the process with clear instructions and progress indicators.

Your job postings automatically display required skills, helping candidates self-assess their fit before applying. This reduces unqualified applications by up to 60%.

Automated Candidate Screening

Save 85% of manual screening time

Stop spending hours reviewing resumes manually. The system automatically scores and ranks candidates based on skill match percentage. Qualified candidates are moved to interview stages automatically, while mismatched applications are filtered appropriately.

Set custom thresholds for different positions - decide what match percentage qualifies candidates for initial screening, phone interviews, or direct advancement to final rounds.

Smart Pipeline Management

Candidates automatically organized by qualification level

Your recruitment pipeline stays organized automatically. High-match candidates appear at the top, medium matches are flagged for review, and low matches are segregated for later consideration or rejection.

Each candidate card displays their skill match percentage, matched skills, missing skills, and recommended next actions. Make hiring decisions faster with all relevant information at your fingertips.
